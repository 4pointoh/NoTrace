How to read this

Appearance dims her top off are the states that must stay consistent in all later art. The strip ladder is a chain (top → shorts → bra → panties), so states grow one per win instead of exploding. Her wins add no state at all — the loss track is pure interleaving, which is why match count (4–7) doesn't touch the art budget.

Ladder rungs carry sharedId: the same rung fired in a different match reuses the same render set, per outfit variant. A butt touch with her top off is one render set whether it happens in Match 2 or Match 3. Only genuinely distinct outfit variants add art.

The pyramid rule: rung k of a per-state ladder can co-exist with at most 4−k strip-ladder picks (slots are finite) — and since the strip ladder needs 3 picks just to reach shorts-off (top, walk, shorts), variants shrink hard as content escalates: butt touch ×3, breast ×2, BJ ×2, sex ×1. The most explicit art is the cheapest to keep consistent.

Narrative flags truth1 gate rungs but never show in ambient art — they cost dialogue, not renders. Pooled pooled events are framed so outfit doesn't show (close-ups, money) — one render set total.
